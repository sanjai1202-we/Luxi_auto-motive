Who we are

A detailing studio built
around one car at a time.

Luxi Automotive Care has been working out of Rangapuram, Vellore since 2019 — washing, correcting, coating and protecting cars for owners who notice the difference between "clean" and actually cared for. No shortcuts, no filler products: every panel is inspected, corrected and finished by hand before it ever sees a coating gun or a roll of film.

From a daily-driven hatchback in for a wash to a Mustang going under XPEL film, the process doesn't change — decontaminate, correct, protect, deliver.

Panels dismantled, film installed right.
